# App Review notes — copy after final device validation

No sign-in, purchase, subscription or demo account is needed. VoiceToRead is an on-device caption reader and language practice app for iPhone and iPad. It does not record internal audio from other apps. This submission is not a medical device, certified interpretation service or Kids Category submission.

1. Open Settings. “Menus and buttons” controls interface language (English/Español). “Spoken language” independently selects English (default), Spanish, French or Korean for the next recording. “Translate to” selects a different target; availability depends on device resources. The compact selectors before recording use the same preferences.
2. In Home → Listen, follow the visible microphone/model preparation action. Allow microphone and, if requested, Speech Recognition. Prepare the indicated Apple language model using Internet. Setup differs between the compatibility recognizer and the modern speech engine.
3. Tap the microphone and speak nearby. Saving audio initially defaults on in new installations, but can be turned off before starting. Pause/Resume retains the session. Stop ends capture and presents Save Session/Discard. Save moves the recovery draft into the saved-session library without changing its identity; Discard deletes its text and recorded audio.
4. Open Saved → saved session → Audio & subtitles. Choose At my pace, play one paragraph, wait for it to stop, then select Previous/Next and tap Play when ready. The same central button replays a finished paragraph. The caption area fills the available height; Playback options expands secondary actions. Correct paragraph saves reviewed text while original audio remains unchanged.
5. Saved initially lists saved sessions by date; All/Favorites excludes recovery drafts. The library options menu opens recovery drafts and Recently deleted; wide iPad uses a sidebar. Swipe a session left (or use its actions button) to show circular favorite/protect and delete controls. Choose Favorite & protect. Favorites filter lists it; deletion of session/audio requires removing protection first and a separate deletion confirmation.
6. Translation requires a compatible modern build on iOS/iPadOS 18+ and Apple language downloads. Choose translation preparation if requested, then open Text & translation to translate and edit saved text. During capture, the language bubble offers Original, Translation and Both; Both pairs confirmed original paragraphs with their translations. The preview is temporary and does not automatically save translations. On iOS 17 the app explains unavailability while captioning/review remain available.
7. Original audio and translated speech share Audio & subtitles with identical transport controls. One Read and listen selection controls text and audio. Both displays labelled cards and plays original then translation per paragraph; At my pace stops after both. Text & translation has no inline playback; its single Audio & subtitles shortcut opens the shared player. Select Original, Translation or Both there. Play/Pause/Resume controls sound. Previous/Next select without auto-playing. Translation speech also works without a recording; a compatible device voice is required. No synthesized audio export is included; automatic spoken translation is provided separately by Conversation. Stop recording starts automatic text translation on compatible builds; progress/retry is visible and Save/Discard remains explicit. Download prompts may appear. Backgrounding suspends translation until foreground while the process remains alive.
8. Language practice offers examples and short temporary pronunciation takes. Optional on-device word recognition is not pronunciation scoring. Voice/model preparation is indicated in the UI.
9. Background continuation is explicit and initially off. With it enabled, the audio background mode supports microphone recording/recognition after switching apps or locking the screen. A call/alarm may still interrupt it. Resume is always explicit. There is no silent background microphone restart.

Privacy policy is accessible in Settings. Sessions/audio are local and excluded from backup; exported copies follow the chosen destination. No developer server, ads or analytics. Recognition accuracy and model availability vary; claims do not promise perfect captions.

Accessibility update: Settings offers Simple mode, visible button names, focused paragraph reading, optional pause vibration and a preparation demo without capture. Write and speak (Simple home, or Captions → Session actions) speaks user-entered text on explicit action, does not translate it and clears it on leaving. Recently deleted retains completed removed sessions locally for 30 days; Restore and Delete permanently are explicit. Draft discard remains permanent. See accessible-experience-checkpoint.md for actual validation; no new accessibility certification is claimed.

Phrase bookmark controls, lists and export selection are retired. Existing local bookmark data is retained for compatibility; whole-session favorites/protection remain available.

Playback comfort: the speaking card shows “Listening now”. The speed menu offers separate original/translation speeds and an optional 0/1/2-second language gap. Language roles and playback mode persist. Paragraph search matches original or current translated text and plays the selected result. Generated voices are not automatically exported. Preparation shows voice availability, which must still be tested offline on the device.

Conversation is the default home, embedded directly without a separate sheet. Choose Me / Other person languages, tap Speak, then Finish and translate; the finalized translation plays aloud. Stop audio and replay remain available; correction is removed from Conversation, while saved transcript editing is unchanged. Write a reply opens an editor in Me's language, regardless of who spoke last; Cancel preserves the message. Conversation always translates, ignoring the retired off preference. Submitting a written reply translates and speaks it. Converse and Listen share header/selector positions; Listen retains original-only reading and its options in Session actions. Home → Listen keeps recording/export workflows separate. Mode switching is disabled during microphone activity, finalization or playback. Each turn starts explicitly; automatic finish remains an optional setting. Temporary conversation creates no recording/session. Leaving/backgrounding cancels active audio/jobs; messages may remain in memory while the home is retained, and are not persisted. Translation requires iOS/iPadOS 18+ with compatible Apple resources. Settings → Home experience → Previous home is a reversible comparison option, not a data migration.

Navigation: completion buttons use a checkmark with the accessible name Done. Save/Discard remain explicit. Settings → Check preparation replaces the permanent Before a meeting or class banner; necessary permission/model warnings remain in Captions.

Capture controls: Save audio, Reduce noise and Background show names and On/Off states before starting. Settings is the normal preferences entry; a separate shortcut remains in fullscreen. When rereading live captions, the circular down arrow returns to live text and resumes following; it has an accessible label.

Language colors: Settings → Language colors provides independent English/Spanish palettes, live preview and reset. Defaults remain English blue/Spanish green in conversation controls, bilingual live cards and playback cards, regardless of translation direction. Names remain visible; high contrast overrides fills. Reading paper affects the surrounding area, not language card colors. This changes presentation only.

Conversation now has two language selectors and two colored turn buttons. Choose a supported pair; tap a speaker button, speak, then finish to translate and play in the other language. Text & translation retains separate saved destinations and corrections. French/Korean device acceptance is a release gate, not certified by simulator fixtures.
