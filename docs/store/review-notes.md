# App Review notes — copy after final device validation

No sign-in, purchase, subscription or demo account is needed. VoiceToRead is an on-device caption reader and language practice app for iPhone and iPad. It does not record internal audio from other apps. This submission is not a medical device, certified interpretation service or Kids Category submission.

1. Open Settings. “Menus and buttons” controls interface language (English/Español). “Spoken language” independently selects English (default) or Spanish for the next recording.
2. In Captions, follow the visible microphone/model preparation action. Allow microphone and, if requested, Speech Recognition. Prepare the indicated Apple language model using Internet. Setup differs between the compatibility recognizer and the modern speech engine.
3. Tap the microphone and speak nearby. Saving audio initially defaults on in new installations, but can be turned off before starting. Pause/Resume retains the session. Stop ends capture and presents Save Session/Discard. Save moves the recovery draft into the saved-session library without changing its identity; Discard deletes its text and recorded audio.
4. Open Sessions → saved session → Audio & subtitles. Choose At my pace, play one paragraph, wait for it to stop, then select Previous/Next and tap Play when ready. The same central button replays a finished paragraph. The caption area fills the available height; Playback options expands secondary actions. Correct paragraph saves reviewed text while original audio remains unchanged.
5. Swipe a session left (or use its actions button) to show circular favorite/protect and delete controls. Choose Favorite & protect. Favorites filter lists it; deletion of session/audio requires removing protection first and a separate deletion confirmation.
6. Translation requires a compatible modern build on iOS/iPadOS 18+ and Apple language downloads. Choose translation preparation if requested, then translate saved text. During capture, the language bubble offers Original, Translation and Both; Both pairs confirmed original paragraphs with their translations. The preview is temporary and does not automatically save translations. On iOS 17 the app explains unavailability while captioning/review remain available.
7. Original audio and translated speech share Audio & subtitles with identical transport controls. One Read and listen selection controls text and audio. Both displays labelled cards and plays original then translation per paragraph; At my pace stops after both. Translation paragraph buttons play inline, with Pause/Resume/Stop, without opening a player sheet. Play/Pause/Resume controls sound. Previous/Next select without auto-playing. Translation speech also works without a recording; a compatible device voice is required. No synthesized audio export is included; automatic spoken translation is provided separately by Speak and translate. Stop recording starts automatic text translation on compatible builds; progress/retry is visible and Save/Discard remains explicit. Download prompts may appear. Backgrounding suspends translation until foreground while the process remains alive.
8. Language practice offers examples and short temporary pronunciation takes. Optional on-device word recognition is not pronunciation scoring. Voice/model preparation is indicated in the UI.
9. Background continuation is explicit and initially off. With it enabled, the audio background mode supports microphone recording/recognition after switching apps or locking the screen. A call/alarm may still interrupt it. Resume is always explicit. There is no silent background microphone restart.

Privacy policy is accessible in Settings. Sessions/audio are local and excluded from backup; exported copies follow the chosen destination. No developer server, ads or analytics. Recognition accuracy and model availability vary; claims do not promise perfect captions.

Accessibility update: Settings offers Simple mode, visible button names, focused paragraph reading, optional pause vibration and a preparation demo without capture. Write a response speaks user-entered text on explicit action, does not translate it and clears it on leaving. Recently deleted retains completed removed sessions locally for 30 days; Restore and Delete permanently are explicit. Draft discard remains permanent. See accessible-experience-checkpoint.md for actual validation; no new accessibility certification is claimed.

Bookmark update: Save bookmark/Remove bookmark is visible beside finalized live paragraphs (including bilingual cards), saved paragraphs, translation paragraphs and the media player. The same original paragraph ID is shared across views. The Bookmarks list returns to saved phrases; this differs from protecting an entire session as a favorite.

Playback comfort: the speaking card shows “Listening now”. The speed menu offers separate original/translation speeds and an optional 0/1/2-second language gap. Language roles and playback mode persist. Paragraph search matches original or current translated text and plays the selected result. Bookmarks support local optional notes and synthesized paragraph previews; neither notes nor generated voices are automatically exported. Preparation shows voice availability, which must still be tested offline on the device.

Speak and translate: from idle Captions or Simple home, open Speak and translate. Choose Automatic (speak Spanish, pause for 3/5 seconds, then hear English) or By button (tap Speak Spanish, then Finished speaking, read both texts and explicitly tap Play English). The mode is remembered; changing it does not start capture. Manual mode does not require holding a button. Microphone finalization always precedes translation and speech. Typing/correction, replay and cancel are available. This mode is temporary and creates no audio file or session; it does not change global recording preferences. Requires iOS/iPadOS 18+ in a compatible build and prepared Apple resources. Leaving/backgrounding cancels, and another turn requires a new tap. Please test actual speech/translation on a device with models prepared.

Capture controls: Save audio, Reduce noise and Background show names and On/Off states before starting. Settings is the normal preferences entry; a separate shortcut remains in fullscreen. When rereading live captions, the circular down arrow returns to live text and resumes following; it has an accessible label.
